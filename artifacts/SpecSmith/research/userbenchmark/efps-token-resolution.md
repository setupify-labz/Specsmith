## What would change these outcomes

Almost every block above is `not-in-catalog`: the part is identified without
ambiguity, SpecSmith simply has no entry for it. Those resolve the day the
corresponding part is added to `gpus.json` / `cpus.json` and a reviewed alias
is added to `efpsHardwareMap.ts` — not before, and never automatically.

The near-miss columns are the reason this is a hand-written table rather than a
similarity function. Matching on digits alone against the real catalog maps the
AMD RX 580 onto an Intel Arc A580, the AMD Ryzen 5 2600 onto an Intel i5-12600K,
and the Intel i5-9600K onto an AMD Ryzen 5 9600X. Each is a different vendor,
and in several cases a different decade.
