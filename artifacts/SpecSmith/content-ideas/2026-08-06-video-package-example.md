# Example run: `{{TARGET}}` = "Build Crate legendary pull"

One worked example of `video-prompt-system-template.md`, to validate
the system before it runs unattended. Real data used throughout: a
legendary-tier Build Crate pull is a top-tier GPU/CPU landing together
(e.g. RTX 5090 + Ryzen 9 9950X3D), the actual rarity tiers and reveal
sequence already live on `/crate`.

## 1. Concept summary
Hook: **The Gamified Dopamine Hit.** Cold open on tension-building B-roll,
then a real Build Crate pull on screen, letting the site's own
sequential per-part reveal and rarity-tier confetti do the payoff —
no need to fake a better result than the real system produces.

## 2. Scene-by-scene shot list

**Shot 1** — `[AI B-ROLL]` — 1.5s
> Extreme macro shot, slow rack-focus pull from a glowing RGB keycap
> in the foreground to a blurred monitor glow in the background.
> Premium dark-mode desk setup, deep purple-to-cyan accent lighting
> reflecting softly off a matte black desk surface. Cinematic, shallow
> depth of field, 4K look, subtle slow-motion (60fps feel).

**Shot 2** — `[SCREEN RECORDING]` — 4s
> specsmithpc.com/crate. Cursor moves to the "Open a Crate" button,
> a beat of hesitation, then click. Let the site's real opening
> animation play — don't skip or speed it up.

**Shot 3** — `[SCREEN RECORDING]` — 6s
> The real sequential per-part reveal as it happens on the live site —
> each category (GPU, CPU, etc.) landing one at a time. Do not cut
> reveals together out of order; the tension is the real pacing.

**Shot 4** — `[AI B-ROLL]` — 1s
> Quick particle/glow transition — a burst of light matching the
> site's own rarity-tier accent color (gold for legendary) — used as a
> whip-transition into the final reveal, not as a replacement for it.

**Shot 5** — `[SCREEN RECORDING]` — 3s
> The final result card on screen: rarity badge, GPU+CPU names, real
> total cost and avg FPS as computed by the site. Hold on this — it's
> the payoff, don't rush past it.

## 3. Kinetic typography captions
- "0:00 — appears at 0:00, holds 1.2s: **"i rolled the dice on a $6,000 pc build"**
- 0:04 — appears on Shot 2 click, holds 0.8s: **"opening..."**
- 0:06-0:11 — one word lands per reveal in Shot 3, synced to each part landing: **"GPU."** / **"CPU."** / (etc., matching real reveal order)
- 0:12 — appears on Shot 5, holds 1.5s, largest text of the video: **"LEGENDARY"** (or whatever tier the real pull actually lands — never pre-write the tier, caption it live)
- 0:13 — appears under the tier text: **[real total cost] · [real avg FPS] fps**

## 4. Audio/sound design cues
- Shot 2 click — subtle mechanical keyboard/mouse-click sound, synced exactly to the on-screen click.
- Shot 2→3 transition — sci-fi UI swoosh.
- Each reveal in Shot 3 — a soft rising "blip" per part landing, pitch stepping up slightly each time (building tension).
- Shot 4 transition — a sharper whoosh matching the particle burst.
- Shot 5 payoff — low bass drop exactly as the rarity badge appears.
- Ambient bed — low-key synth pad, tension-building, no strong beat until the bass drop.

## Notes on validating this system
- Every number/result in this example is a placeholder for whatever
  the real pull actually produces when filmed — the template
  deliberately does NOT pre-decide the outcome, since faking a better
  pull than the real gacha system produces would be exactly the kind
  of "mockup" the standing content rule forbids.
- Total B-roll time: 2.5s out of an ~15.5s runtime — well under the
  guideline that B-roll stays texture/transition, not the emotional
  core.
